## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a contemporary neo-naïve hand drawing: loose slightly shaky coloured outlines, uneven marker and coloured-pencil marks, rounded irregular organic shapes and slightly clumsy proportions. Keep a small subject and abundant warm cream paper. Colours are soft with a few brighter accents taken from the source. It is not a polished vector, a filled realistic scene, a 3D render, a complex shadow study or a formal commercial title.
